## 👋 About me

I'm **Hakeem Adekunle**, a biostatistician and data scientist interested in **statistical methodology, public health, health actuarial analytics, and machine learning**. My research interests include Bayesian methods, measurement error, causal inference, evidence synthesis, and health disparities.

I use quantitative approaches to turn complex data into interpretable evidence for healthcare research and decision-making. I welcome professional opportunities and research collaborations.
